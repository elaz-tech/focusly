FOCUSLY — GOOGLE PLAY LISTING
================================

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 characters max)
-------------------------------------
Lock your phone and stay focused. Only calls get through until time is up.


FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 characters max)
--------------------------------------
Can't stop checking your phone? Focusly locks it for you.

Set a timer from 1 to 120 minutes and your phone stays locked until it
expires. No feeds, no chats, no "just one quick check". Incoming and
outgoing phone calls always work, so you stay reachable for what truly
matters — and nothing else.

HOW IT WORKS
- Pick a duration (1–120 minutes) and tap START FOCUSING.
- Your phone locks: notifications are silenced, other apps are blocked.
- When the timer ends, your phone unlocks automatically.

BUILT TO KEEP YOU HONEST
- Iron Curtain overlay: a full-screen barrier appears if you try to leave.
- Enforced mode: opening other apps or system panels snaps you back.
- Self-healing watchdog: the lock relaunches itself if the system stops it.
- Tamper-proof timer: changing the system clock can't end a session early.
- Emergency escape (optional): a long-press hatch for genuine emergencies.
  Disable it in Settings for a stricter lock.

STAYS RELIABLE ON YOUR DEVICE
- Guided setup for Do Not Disturb, overlay, and accessibility permissions.
- Battery-optimization exemption and auto-start help for Xiaomi, Oppo,
  Vivo, and Huawei devices.

PERMISSIONS — WHY WE NEED THEM
- Do Not Disturb access: silences notifications during a lock. Calls pass through.
- Display over other apps: shows the focus barrier if you try to switch apps.
- Accessibility service: detects app-switching and blocks bypass attempts
  while a lock is active. Never collects or transmits any data.
- Ignore battery optimizations (optional): keeps long sessions alive.

PRIVACY FIRST
Zero data collection. Everything runs locally on your device. No accounts,
no tracking, no ads.
Privacy policy: https://supporteaf.netlify.app/pages/privacy_policy_focusly.html

PLEASE NOTE
- Like all Android lockers, Focusly can't block a hardware-level Safe Mode boot.
- Focusly is Android-only.

Less scrolling, more living. Install Focusly and take back your attention.
